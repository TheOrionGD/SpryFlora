1. PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION
================================================================================
Botanical nurturing, environmental education, and domestic horticulture instill
crucial developmental competencies in children and novice gardeners: patience,
ecological responsibility, scientific observation, and bio-awareness. However,
conventional plant care paradigms suffer from fundamental systemic bottlenecks:

1.1. Asynchronous Feedback Loops & Cognitive Detachment:
     Biological plant growth operates on prolonged temporal cycles (90 to 180
     days). For youth accustomed to instant digital feedback loops, the
     inability to perceive daily metabolic responses leads to disengagement,
     erratic watering cadences, and high plant mortality rates.

1.2. Diagnostic Asymmetry & Pathology Blindspots:
     Early symptoms of botanical distress (chlorosis, tip scorch, powdery mildew,
     nitrogen deficiency, root rot) present subtle visual signatures. Novice
     caregivers frequently misdiagnose symptoms (e.g., over-watering a plant
     suffering from fungal drainage blockage), exacerbating plant demise.

1.3. Absence of Quantified Milestone Verification:
     Traditional home gardening lacks structured achievement validation.
     Children and students invest months of labor without verifiable credentials,
     gamified milestone tracking, or automated progress reinforcement.

1.4. Network Dependency of Modern Smart Gardening Tools:
     Existing agricultural and botanical applications heavily mandate continuous
     broadband connectivity, rendering them inoperative in outdoor school
     gardens, remote botanical field excursions, and low-connectivity regions.

2. ABSTRACT & RESEARCH SUMMARY
================================================================================
SpryFlora is an offline-first, multimodal AI-assisted mobile botanical care
companion engineered in Flutter 3.x and Dart 3.x. The platform bridges biological
plant care with gamified human-computer interaction (HCI) through four core
technical innovations:

1. Multi-Factor Mathematical Health Modeling (SHARP Engine):
   A real-time evaluation algorithm that processes hydration intervals,
   photoperiod compliance, care streak consistency, and lifecycle stage
   stability into a deterministic vitality index ($H \in [0, 100]$).

2. Dual-Engine Multimodal Vision Diagnostic Pipeline:
   A hybrid pathology pipeline that combines cloud-hosted Google Gemini Vision
   models (Gemini Flash Lite / 3.5 Flash) with a local deterministic heuristic
   rule engine, guaranteeing sub-second diagnostic failover during offline states.

3. 2.5D Isometric Interactive Digital Garden:
   A custom canvas-rendered 4x4 floating isometric garden island featuring
   procedural botanical growth stages, live wind sway physics, and particle
   celebration shaders.

4. Native Operating System Subsystem Synchronization:
   Bi-directional bridge linking local application state to Android AppWidgets
   (`FloraWidgetProvider`) for glanceable home screen telemetry, exact alarm
   notification scheduling, and 3.0x pixel-ratio certificate rasterization.

5. PROPOSED SYSTEM: SPRYFLORA
================================================================================
SpryFlora introduces an integrated ecosystem combining deterministic edge
computations, multimodal vision intelligence, and gamified bio-feedback:

- Offline-First Architecture: All core services (PlantRepository, UserService,
  PlantHealthEngine, ExcelService, CertificateCaptureService) execute locally
  via SharedPreferences and sandboxed file storage.
- Deterministic Stage Transitions: Plant progression is calculated dynamically:
  $$\text{Progress} = \min\left(1.0, \frac{t_{\text{current}} - t_{\text{planted}}}{T_{\text{lifespan}}}\right)$$
  Classifying plants across four stages: Seed (0%-25%), Sprout (25%-50%),
  Growing Plant (50%-99%), Fully Grown Plant (100%).
- Real-time Multiplatform Camera Integration: Native camera capture across
  Android, iOS, Web, and Windows Desktop (`ms-windows-camera:` bridge).
- Android AppWidget Provider: Glanceable live widget updating urgent watering
  counts, health metrics, and photoperiod progress on the user's home screen.

7. ADVANTAGES & MATHEMATICAL INNOVATION (SHARP)
================================================================================

7.1. The SHARP Multi-Factor Botanical Vitality Engine:
     The SHARP model evaluates five fundamental botanical dimensions:
     - S: Sunlight & Photoperiod Exposure Index
     - H: Hydration & Soil Moisture Retention
     - A: Adherence & Care Streak Consistency
     - R: Rate of Stage Progression & Maturation
     - P: Pathology & Stress Penalty Classification

7.2. Mathematical Formulation:
     The overall health score $H_{\text{overall}} \in [0, 100]$ is computed as:

     $$H_{\text{overall}} = \text{clamp}\left(0, 100, \sum_{i=1}^{4} w_i \cdot S_i - P_{\text{penalty}}\right)$$

     Where:
     - $w_1 = 0.40$ (Hydration Weight $S_{\text{hyd}}$)
     - $w_2 = 0.35$ (Sunlight Weight $S_{\text{sun}}$)
     - $w_3 = 0.15$ (Consistency Weight $S_{\text{con}}$)
     - $w_4 = 0.10$ (Progression Weight $S_{\text{prog}}$)

7.3. Sub-Factor Mathematical Derivations:

     (a) Hydration Score ($S_{\text{hyd}}$):
         Let $\Delta t_{\text{water}} = t_{\text{target\_water}} - t_{\text{current}}$ (days).
         $$S_{\text{hyd}} = \begin{cases} 
         \text{clamp}(80, 100, S_{\text{prev}} + 15) & \text{if Watered Today = True} \\
         \text{clamp}(20, 100, 100 - (|\Delta t_{\text{water}}| \times 8)) & \text{if } \Delta t_{\text{water}} < 0 \text{ (Overdue)} \\
         \min(S_{\text{prev}}, 88) & \text{if } \Delta t_{\text{water}} = 0 \text{ (Due Today)} \\
         \text{clamp}(70, 100, S_{\text{prev}} + 2) & \text{otherwise}
         \end{cases}$$

     (b) Sunlight Compliance Score ($S_{\text{sun}}$):
         Let $R_{\text{sun}} = \frac{h_{\text{logged}}}{h_{\text{target}}}$.
         $$S_{\text{sun}} = \begin{cases}
         100 & \text{if } 0.8 \le R_{\text{sun}} \le 1.2 \\
         \text{clamp}\left(30, 80, \frac{R_{\text{sun}}}{0.8} \times 80\right) & \text{if } R_{\text{sun}} < 0.8 \text{ (Under-exposed)} \\
         92 & \text{if } R_{\text{sun}} > 1.2 \text{ (Excess Light)}
         \end{cases}$$

     (c) Care Consistency Streak ($S_{\text{con}}$):
         Calculated over a rolling 7-day observation window $W_7$:
         $$S_{\text{con}} = \text{clamp}\left(40, 100, \frac{N_{\text{watered\_days}} + N_{\text{sunlit\_days}}}{2 \times |W_7|} \times 100\right)$$

     (d) Stage Progression Stability ($S_{\text{prog}}$):
         $$S_{\text{prog}} = \begin{cases}
         100 & \text{if } \text{ageInDays} \ge \text{lifespanDays} \text{ (Completed)} \\
         90  & \text{if } \text{ageInDays} > 0 \\
         75  & \text{otherwise}
         \end{cases}$$

7.4. Status Classification Bounds:
     - $H_{\text{overall}} \ge 90 \implies \text{🌟 Thriving}$
     - $75 \le H_{\text{overall}} < 90 \implies \text{🌿 Optimal}$
     - $S_{\text{hyd}} < 55 \implies \text{💧 Under-watered (High Priority Alert)}$
     - $S_{\text{sun}} < 55 \implies \text{☀️ Needs Sunlight (Photoperiod Deficit)}$
     - Otherwise $\implies \text{⚠️ Stressed (Multi-Factor Degradation)}$

8. DETAILED MODULE DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

8.1. `PlantRepository` (`lib/services/plant_repository.dart`):
     - Acts as the reactive single source of truth implementing `ChangeNotifier`.
     - Maintains in-memory lists `List<PlantModel>` and `List<DailyCheckinModel>`.
     - Handles CRUD operations with automated JSON serialization to `SharedPreferences`.
     - Emits change notifications to trigger reactive UI re-renders.

8.2. `AIService` (`lib/services/ai_service.dart`):
     - Orchestrates multimodal leaf pathology diagnostics and botanical Q&A.
     - Dual-model cloud routing: `gemini-flash-lite-latest` (primary) and
       `gemini-3.5-flash` / `gemini-1.5-flash` (secondary).
     - Encodes image files to RFC 4648 Base64 byte arrays.
     - Built-in heuristic fallback engine analyzing telemetry when offline.

8.3. `PlantHealthEngine` (`lib/services/plant_health_engine.dart`):
     - Executes the mathematical SHARP 4-factor formula.
     - Produces structured `PlantHealthReport` with status, warnings, and tips.

8.4. `WateringService` (`lib/services/watering_service.dart`):
     - Processes daily check-in forms and updates plant hydration timestamps.
     - Projects the next watering target date based on species intervals.

8.5. `ExcelService` (`lib/services/excel_service.dart`):
     - Decodes `species_data.json` and optional XLSX botanical databases.
     - Supports runtime dynamic registration of newly discovered species.

8.6. `WidgetSyncService` (`lib/services/widget_sync_service.dart`):
     - Serializes high-priority plant health, watering, and photoperiod metrics.
     - Pushes payloads to Android `FloraWidgetProvider` via `home_widget`.

8.7. `CertificateCaptureService` (`lib/services/certificate_capture_service.dart`):
     - Locates `RenderRepaintBoundary` objects from the widget hierarchy.
     - Rasterizes widgets into 3.0x pixel-ratio PNG byte buffers.
     - Saves files to sandboxed storage and invokes OS native share sheets.

8.8. `NotificationService` (`lib/services/notification_service.dart`):
     - Configures Android notification channel `plant_watering_channel`.
     - Schedules high-priority notifications with alarms for watering reminders.

11. IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS & CRYPTOGRAPHIC PIPELINE
================================================================================

11.1. High-Resolution Certificate Rasterization Pipeline:
      To ensure official milestone diplomas are suitable for physical printing,
      SpryFlora implements a high-density rasterization pipeline directly from
      Flutter's render tree:
      
      ```dart
      RenderRepaintBoundary boundary = key.currentContext.findRenderObject();
      ui.Image image = await boundary.toImage(pixelRatio: 3.0);
      ByteData byteData = await image.toByteData(format: ui.ImageByteFormat.png);
      Uint8List pngBytes = byteData.buffer.asUint8List();
      ```

11.2. Certificate Verification & Tamper-Resistance:
      - Each generated certificate embeds a unique cryptographic reference ID:
        `ID: SF-{CRC32/SHA256(PlantID + PlantingDate + Lifespan)}`
      - Features gold foil procedural stamps (`certificate_approved_stamp.png`),
        botanical filigree corner borders (`botanical_corner_leaves.dart`), and
        formal Cinzel & Cormorant Garamond typography.

11.3. Local Sandbox & Zero-Trust Privacy:
      - All child profile attributes (age, school, name) are strictly stored
        inside the application sandbox directory (`getApplicationDocumentsDirectory()`).
      - Check-in photographs are stored in isolated sub-directories without
        external gallery indexing until explicitly exported by the user.
      - Outgoing API calls communicate exclusively via HTTPS TLS 1.3 to Google
        endpoints with zero third-party telemetry or advertising SDKs.
      - API keys are injected via compile-time Dart environment variables
        (`String.fromEnvironment('GEMINI_API_KEY')`), eliminating hardcoded keys.
